# AIOS Surfaced Quick Win Lane — Phase 6

This package installs a governed Quick Win surfacing pass.

Expected outcome:

- Best Next Actions remain the top execution-ranked tasks.
- Quick Wins become a separate capped lane of up to 5 tasks.
- Quick Wins do not overlap with current BNA winners.
- The Notion Quick Wins view no longer depends on `Execution Rank`.

After installing, create/check the Notion checkbox property `Surfaced Quick Win` and update the Quick Wins view filter to use that property.
